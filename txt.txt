# match

Graph matching algorithms.

## stable-marriage

The typical Gale-Shapely algorithm.

> Given a bipartite graph and a preference list for each node, return a list of bipartite pairs such that all nodes are matched.

Constraints:

- The bipartite graph has an equal number of nodes.
- Each preference list is complete, that is, contains a full list of nodes in the other graph.

## good-marriage

A variation of the stable marriage problem where some people are good and some people are bad. This reduces to a quartile matching problem where tow groups of nodes can match with only two other nodes, and vice versa. This is also reducable to the forbidden marriage problem.

## forbidden-marriage

A variation of the stable marriage problem where some matchings are forbidden.

## indifferent-marriage

A variation of the stable marriage problem where preferences are allowed to have equal ratings.

## npointsinpairs

Connect `n` points in pairs. An extremely important function in Quantum Field Theory.
